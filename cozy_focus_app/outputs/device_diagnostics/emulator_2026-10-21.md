# Emulator instability — 2026-10-21

## What happened

Two emulator launches; the third was **not attempted**. On both, the emulator
process disappeared during the landscape sweep and `adb devices` went empty.
This is not the app taking the foreground: the device itself is gone.

| attempt | launch | result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | default AVD RAM (2G), swiftshader | booted, then the process was gone during the landscape sweep; `no device attached` |
| 2 | `-memory 1536 -no-boot-anim` | booted (`sys.boot_completed=1`), app launched, sweep refused: `nothing painted after rotating`; then the process was gone |
| 3 | after `gradlew --stop` | not retried |

Host free physical memory:

- before stopping the Gradle daemon: **1,586,120 KB (~1.5 GB)** of 16,370,568 KB
- after `gradlew --stop`: **2,250,452 KB (~2.2 GB)**
- the AVD's `hw.ramSize` is 2 GB

**The cause is a suspicion, not a measurement.** Free host memory is close to
the AVD's own footprint, which would explain a kill, but nothing captured here
identifies an OOM: there is no `emulator` log, no host event-log entry, and no
`adb` message beyond the device vanishing. "Not enough memory" is the leading
hypothesis; it is not proven by this evidence.

The existing `anr_2026-10-12.txt` is a **SystemUI / NavigationBar0** ANR, not the
app: `ANR in com.android.systemui`, reason `Input dispatching timed out
(NavigationBar0 ...)`. That matches the known software-rendering starvation, not
an app freeze.

## What is verified without the device

- `com.yuhangzhu295.cozyfocus` was installed on the device before it vanished.
- The app launched and painted; the landscape tool reached the rotation step.
- Widget-level landscape coverage (`screen_size_and_text_sweep_test.dart`) passes
  at 360x800, 393x852, 412x915, 800x360, 852x393 and 915x412, 1.0x and 1.3x text.
  That is **not** a substitute for the device screenshots the brief asks for.

## Status

`LANDSCAPE_DEVICE_SWEEP = BLOCKED_HOST_MEMORY`.

Do not restart the emulator repeatedly on this host; it consumes the last ~2 GB
and dies during rotation. Device-only items (landscape screenshots, live
collection walk, animation recording) stay BLOCKED until the host has enough
free memory for the AVD's 2 GB plus the app.
